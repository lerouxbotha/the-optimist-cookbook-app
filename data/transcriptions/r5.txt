@ Sunday, Bloody Sunday | drinks | 61
s: 4
t: 5 minutes
> This take on a Bloody Mary is a staple in our house… especially on Sunday’s. We make a big jug that’ll last till lunch!
# You’ll need…
- 1 lt Tomato Juice
- 1 pt Vodka per serving (100ml for a jug)
- 25 ml Fresh Lime Juice
- 25 ml fresh Lemon Juice
- 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- 25 ml Tabasco Sauce
- 2 tsp Tajin (plus extra for the glass rim)
- 25 ml pickle juice
- 25 ml Balsamic Vinegar
- Honey (for the glass rim)
. Rim a tall glass with honey and Tajin.
. Mix all ingredients in a shaker/food processor with ice.
. Strain into a chilled glass and serve with celery stick, pickles and olives.

@ Negroni | drinks | 67
s: 1
t: 2 minutes
> A timeless Italian aperitif — bitter, bold, and beautifully balanced. I really like this cocktail… and I might be alone on this island.
# You’ll need…
- 1 pt gin
- 1 pt Campari
- 1 pt sweet vermouth
- ice
- orange peel for garnish
. Fill a rocks glass with ice to chill while you prepare the drink.
. In a mixing glass, add the gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth. Add ice and stir gently for 20–30 seconds until well chilled.
. Discard the ice from the serving glass. Strain the cocktail into the glass over fresh ice.
. Express the oils from a twist of orange peel over the drink, then drop it in as garnish.
! For a smoother sip, try equal parts over a large ice cube, or for a modern twist, swap the gin for mezcal or prosecco (a Negroni Sbagliato).

@ Michelada | drinks | 68
s: 1
t: 5 minutes
> A smoky, spicy Mexican beer cocktail with mezcal and a chilli-lime kick. Perfect for hot days, smoky braais, or spicy food pairings!
# You’ll need…
- 1 pt mezcal
- 1 pt freshly squeezed lime juice
- 1/2 pt honey
- 5 dashes Tabasco sauce
- 5 dashes balsamic vinegar
- 3 dashes Worcestershire sauce
- 1 bottle Sol or Corona, chilled
- 2 pt tomato juice
- Honey (for the glass rim)
- 2 tsp Tajin (plus extra for the glass rim)
. Rim a tall glass with honey and Tajin.
. In a shaker filled with ice, combine the mezcal, lime juice, honey, Tabasco, Balsamic Vinegar, tomato juice, and Worcestershire sauce. Shake briefly to chill.
. Strain the mixture into the prepared glass over fresh ice. Top with chilled lager. Garnish with a lime wedge.
! Keep the remaining beer nearby to top up as needed.
