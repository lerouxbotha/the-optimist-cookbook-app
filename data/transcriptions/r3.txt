- 1 kg lamb stewing meat or lamb shanks (skenkels), cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- flour to coat the meat
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ground allspice (with flour for meat)
- 15 ml (2 tsp) finely grated ginger
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp paprika
- A good chug of Worsthershire sauce
- 2 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes (or 6 ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped)
- 1 tsp sugar (to balance the acidity of the tomatoes)
- 250 ml water
- 4 medium potatoes, peeled and quartered
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Fresh coriander or parsley, to garnish
. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Coat the lamb in flour and allspice mix. Add the lamb pieces to the pot and brown them well on all sides. Remove and set aside.
. In the same pot, reduce the heat and sauté the onions until soft and golden. Add the garlic, ginger, and cook for another minute. Stir in the coriander and paprika. Cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

. Add the potatoes in the last 30–40 minutes of cooking, stirring occasionally. Add more water if needed to prevent sticking.

> A thick, creamy Middle Eastern strained yoghurt cheese — tangy, versatile, and delicious as a dip or spread
- 500 g full-fat plain Greek-style yoghurt (unsweetened)
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp dried chilli flakes
- ½ tsp black pepper
. In a bowl, stir the salt, chilli and black pepper into the yoghurt until fully combined.
. Line a fine mesh sieve with cheesecloth, muslin, or a clean kitchen towel and place over a bowl. Spoon the yoghurt into the cloth, gather the edges, and twist gently to enclose.

! To make labneh balls, roll the thickened labneh into small balls and store in a jar filled with olive oil and herbs — great for mezze platters! Also great to use in the Cilbir recipe on page 29.
