@ Marikie’s Koeksisters | sweet | 51
s: Makes 24-30 koeksisters
t: 45 min (plus overnight rest) + 30 min cooking
> Another Marikie recipe. My cousin’s husband is obsessed with Koeksisters, so my mom gave them this recipe to make koeksisters in London, where they live. We tested this recipe together… and it is a really cool recipe! Traditional South African syrup-drenched plaits — sweet, crisp, and deliciously sticky.
# For the syrup
- 325 ml (1½ cups) water
- 1 kg white sugar
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1 lemon (rind and juice)
- 6–7 slices raw ginger
. Combine all the syrup ingredients in a saucepan. Bring to the boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
. Once boiling, reduce to medium heat and simmer for 5 minutes without stirring. Remove from heat, strain to remove solids, and transfer to a glass bowl. Refrigerate overnight.
# For the dough
- 750 ml (3 cups) cake flour
- 30 ml (6 tsp) baking powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 56 g (4 tbsp) butter, room temperature
- 2 eggs
- 250 ml (1 cup) milk
- Vegetable oil, for deep frying (±750 ml)
. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Rub the butter into the dry ingredients using your fingertips until it resembles breadcrumbs.
. In a separate bowl, beat the eggs and milk lightly. Gradually add to the dry ingredients, mixing continuously until a soft, pliable dough forms. Knead on a floured surface until smooth. Cover and let rest for at least 2 hours or overnight.
. Roll the rested dough out to about ¼ inch (5 mm) thick on a lightly oiled surface. Cut into strips about 10 cm x 1.5 cm. Twist or braid each strip, pinching the ends to seal.
. Heat the oil in a deep fryer or large pot to 180°C. Fry the koeksisters in batches, turning until golden brown (2–3 minutes per side).
. Remove with a slotted spoon and immediately dip them into the cold syrup. Coat thoroughly. Let cool on a wire rack.
. Best enjoyed once cooled and sticky. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for several days or freeze for longer storage. If frozen, allow to sit at room temperature for 15 minutes before serving.

@ Easy Peasy Baklava | sweet | 52
s:
t:
> I love a good baklava. And this recipe is so easy and delicious… you’ll make a batch every week.
# For the Baklava
- 250 gr raw cashews
- 50 gr pistachio nuts
- 1 roll phyllo pastry, room temperature
- 250 gr Ghee / Clarified Butter
- 100 gr sugar
. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease a deep baking tray.
. In a food processor, grind the cashews and 40gr of the pistachios with the sugar until rough.
. Place a few sheets of phyllo pastry in the baking dish. Brush with some ghee, then layer with nuts. Repeat this twice. Cover with rest of the ground up pistachios.
. Cut into three rows, and then diagonally. Bake for 10 minutes. Once removed, let the baklava cool down.

@ No-Bake Fridge Cheesecake | sweet | 53
s: 6 (or just 1, in Lua’s case)
t: 4 hours (including setting in fridge)
> Our goddaughter, Lua, is a big fan of Cheesecake. This is a really easy no-bake cheesecake that anyone can pull off!
# For the Crust
- 1 packet (200 g) Tennis biscuits, finely crushed
- 75 ml butter, melted
. Mix the crushed Tennis biscuits with the melted butter. Press the mixture firmly into the base of a springform (loose-bottom) pan. Refrigerate while you prepare the filling.
# For Filling
- 250 ml Cremora (coffee creamer)
- 125 ml cold water
- 1 can (397 g) condensed milk
- 250 g creamed cottage cheese
- 15 ml gelatin
- 30 ml cold water (to bloom the gelatine)
- 30 ml boiling water (to dissolve the gelatine)
- 125 ml lemon juice
- 250 ml cream (or imitation cream), whipped to stiff peaks
. In a large bowl, whisk together the Cremora and cold water until smooth. Add the condensed milk and cottage cheese, and whisk until well combined.
. Sprinkle the gelatine over 30 ml cold water and let it stand for 10 minutes to bloom. Add 30 ml boiling water and stir until the gelatine is fully dissolved. Stir the gelatine mixture into the Cremora filling.
. Stir in the lemon juice until fully incorporated. Gently fold in the whipped cream until smooth and airy.
. Pour the filling into the prepared crust. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or until set.
. Just before serving, top the cheesecake with lightly mashed fresh raspberries or fruit of your choice.

@ Jan Ellis Pudding | sweet | 54
s: 8 pieces
t: 45 min
> Jan Ellis Pudding is such a staple in a South African inspired menu. This one works every time!
# For the cake
- 5 ml bicarbonate of soda
- 375 ml self-raising flour
- 2 eggs
- 25 ml very soft butter
- 125 ml milk
- 125 ml white sugar
- 30 ml fine apricot jam
- 1 ml salt
. Preheat oven to 180°C. Spray a heatproof dish with a volume of 2 liters.
. Dissolve the bicarbonate of soda in the milk. Mix the rest of the dough ingredients. Stir in the soda-milk mixture and mix very well.
. Pour the dough into the prepared dish and bake for 30 minutes.
# For the syrup
- 250 ml water
- 250 ml butter
- 5 ml vanilla essence
- 250 ml cream
- 250 ml white sugar
. Meanwhile, combine all the sauce ingredients and bring to a boil.
. Pour a little of the warm sauce over the warm pudding as soon as it comes out of the oven. Let the pudding stand for a while so that the sauce can soak in.
. Serve lukewarm with thin custard or cream.

@ Apple or Pear Pie | sweet | 55
s: 8 pieces
t: 45 min
> You can make this with canned apple or pear pieces… it's delicious either way!
# For the cake
- 140 gr self raising flour
- 2 ml salt
- 105 gr castor sugar
- 125ml butter, room temp
- 385 gr (1 can) apple or pear
- 2 large eggs
- 125ml milk
- 15ml sugar
- 3ml cinnamon
. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease a pie dish (23cm).
. Cream butter & sugar. Add the eggs and beat until light and fluffy. Sift in flour and salt and fold into the mixture. Add the milk and mix well.
. Turn into the greased pie dish. Arrange apple/pear pieces in the pie dish. Mix the cinnamon and sugar and sprinkle over the apple/pear pieces.
. Bake for 30 minutes.
# For the syrup
- 50gr sugar
- 45 ml milk
- 5ml vanilla essence
. Meanwhile, mix the ingredients for the syrup into a small pot on a medium heat until well combined.
. Pur the syrup over the pie as it comes out of the oven. Serve either hot or cold with marscopone.

@ Lemon Drizzle Cake | sweet | 56
s: 10 slices
t: 50 min
> This is one of my mom’s go-to recipes when she has guests over for coffee.
# For the cake
- 250 g unsalted butter (plus extra for greasing)
- 250 g caster sugar
- 4 medium eggs
- 250 g self-raising flour
- Zest of 3 lemons (reserve the juice for the drizzle)
. Preheat oven to 160°C. Grease a loaf tin and line with baking paper.
. Combine all cake ingredients in a large bowl and beat until smooth.
. Spoon mixture into the tin, smooth out the top, and bake for 40-50 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean.
# For the drizzle
- 100 g icing sugar
- Juice of 1.5 lemons (adjust based on size and juiciness)
. While the cake is still warm, poke holes all over with a skewer or knife.
. Mix icing sugar with lemon juice to form a thin paste. Pour the lemon drizzle over the cake, allowing it to soak in.

@ Ultimate Chocolate Brownies | sweet | 57
s: 10 slices
t: 50 min
> This is the ultimate chocolate brownie recipe.
# For the cake
- 140gr butter, melted
- 4 large eggs
- 5 ml vanilla essence
- 350gr castor sugar
- 75 gr cocoa
- 200 gr chocolate, roughly cut
- pinch of salt
- sea salt for garnish
. Preheat oven to 160°C. Grease a 20cm x 20cm baking tray, and line with baking paper.
. Beat eggs, sugar, and vanilla essence until smooth. Mix in the butter. Sift in flour, cocoa and salt. Once mixed, add roughly cut chocolate pieces and mix in.
. Deposit mixture into the prepped baking tray. Top with generous amount of sea salt flakes.
. Bake for 45-55 minutes. The brownie is baked when you place a tooth pick in the brownie and it comes out clean. Turn over on a cooling rack.
# For the ganache
- 75 ml cream
- 100 gr chocolate
. Mix the ingredients for the ganache in a small pot over a medium heat until combined.
. Pour over the baked brownie and let cool. cut into squares and enjoy!
