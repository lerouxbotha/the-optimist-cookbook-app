@ The Clark Kent Calzone | padkos | 8
s: 4
t: 2 hours 15 min
> This was the very first dish I made in the food truck. Luca Castiglione, who owned the truck previously and from whom we bought it, was my guide to this recipe, which he used to sell from the truck - which was previously called Limoncello. I put my own spin on the filling for the calzone, by making a Biltong and Brinjal filling!
# For the calzone dough
- 310 ml water
- 1 tsp salt
- 375 g flour (3 cups)
- 2 tsp dry yeast (or mix active yeast with water)
. Mix all the ingredients together and knead the dough thoroughly.
. Place the dough in a greased bowl and cover with a damp cloth. Let it rise for 1 hour.
. Roll out the risen dough and form small balls. Place the balls on a baking tray and allow to rise for another hour.
# For the classic Italian filling
- 250 ml ricotta cheese (1 cup)
- 250 ml mozzarella cheese, grated (1 cup)
- 250 ml salami, chopped (1 cup)
- 250 ml ham, chopped (1 cup)
# For the Free State Calzone filling
- 250 ml ricotta cheese (1 cup)
- 250 ml mozzarella cheese, grated (1 cup)
- 1 eggplant, diced and deep-fried
- 250 ml biltong, chopped (1 cup)
- 1/2 cup ponzu sauce
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
. Combine the ingredients for your chosen filling and mix well.
# For the Tomato Sauce
- 1 can of tomatoes
- A few basil leaves
- A generous drizzle of olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
. Mix all the ingredients together and set aside.
# For the Calzone
. Preheat the oven to 240°C.
. Roll out a dough ball and place a handful of filling in the center.
. Fold the dough over the filling, seal the edges, and cut into a half-moon shape.
. Place on a greased baking tray and drizzle a small amount of tomato sauce over the top.
. Bake for 10 minutes or until golden brown.

# For the Hotdogs
- 4 cheese griller frankfurters
- 4 hotdog rolls
- 200 ml honey
- 1 cup sriracha sauce
# For the Coleslaw
- ½ head of cabbage, finely shredded
- 2 carrots, grated
- 1 cup mayonnaise
. In a large bowl, combine the cabbage and grated carrots. Stir in the mayonnaise until evenly coated.
. Mix the honey and sriracha sauce. Brush the mixture over the frankfurters and grill or fry until caramelised.
. Place each glazed frankfurter into hotdog roll. Add a generous serving of coleslaw on top.
# For the Mayonnaise (approx. 250 ml)
- 2 egg yolks
- 200 ml olive oil
- 1 tsp mustard
- 15 ml fresh lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
. Ensure all ingredients are at room temperature. Lightly whisk the egg yolks, then add salt and pepper to taste.
. Add the mustard and lemon juice, whisking until combined.
. Slowly add the olive oil in small amounts while whisking continuously until the mayonnaise is thick and creamy. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.

@ Taco Whiz | padkos | 11

t: 1 hour
> As part of the cooking show, I cooked with some of my food heroes in and around the Western Cape. My friend, Chef Henry Vigar, shared this Tuna Tacos with burnt corn, chimichuri & smoked aubergine puree recipe in the show and now I’m sharing it with you!
# For the taco dough
- 1 onion cut into rings
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 cup whole wheat flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- Salt to taste
- 1 cup of water
. Burn the onions until black, processed to a powder in the food processor.
. Add all the ingredients in a food processor and mix until a dough forms. Remove and let rest for 15 minutes under a wet cloth.
. Make palm size balls and roll out very thin. In a dry pan, fry for about two minutes on each side and keep aside.
# For the eggplant puree
- 5 eggplants, roasted over open flames
- 1 onion cut up and pan-fried
- 3 cloves of garlic, chopped and pan-fried
. Burn the eggplant over an open flame. You can also do it on the grill. Remove the peel.
. Add the pan-fried onions and garlic to the aubergine in a food processor and blend. Keep aside.
# For the Tuna
- 2 pieces of fresh tuna
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil
- 1 spring onion, chopped
- 1/2 cup ponzu sauce
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
. Fry tuna on both sides for about one minute.
. Mix together the ingredients for the marinade. Slice the tuna. Let the tuna rest in the marinade until serving.
# For the chimichurri
- 1 cup fresh Italian parsley
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/3 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/4 cup cilantro
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 3/4 teaspoon dry chilies
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon of salt
. Blend all the ingredients in a food processor until fine.
# For the extras
- 300g assorted small tomatoes, sliced and marinated in a basil and olive oil mixture
- 1 red onion very thinly sliced
- 1 head of lettuce, finely chopped
- 2 corns, boiled and fried and de-stalked
- Coriander for garnish
- Onion ash for garnish
# How do you put it all together?
. Layer in this order: tacos, eggplant puree, tomatoes, lettuce, onions, tuna, corn, onion ash, and finish with coriander & chimichurri.

@ Butter Chicken | padkos | 13
s: 4
t: 1 hour
> One of my favourite humans I met on MasterChef was the season 1 runner-up, Sue-Ann Allen. We cooked together in the Bo-Kaap and made Butter Chicken Rotti’s as part of Padkos. It was a fun shoot and this Butter Chicken recipe is a staple in our house.
# For the Butter chicken
- 600 g chicken breasts, diced
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp tandoori masala
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp plain yogurt
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 3 tsp fresh ginger, finely chopped
- 3 tsp fresh garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tsp paprika
- 400 ml tomato passata
- 100 g butter
- 250 ml cream
- 1 cup fresh coriander, torn
- Additional salt, to taste
. Combine the chicken, garam masala, tandoori masala, cumin powder, salt, yogurt, and oil. Marinate for a few hours (or overnight).
. Fry the marinated chicken in a non-stick pan with the oil until almost cooked. Remove from heat and set aside.
. In the same pan, heat the cooking oil and sauté the onion until soft and translucent. Add the paprika, garlic, and ginger, cooking gently until fragrant.
. Stir in the tomato pasata and simmer for about 20 minutes, until the oil begins to separate from the sauce.
. Add the chicken pieces back to the sauce along with the butter and cream. Cook for a few minutes until heated through. Stir in the fresh coriander and increase the heat until the sauce just begins to bubble.

@ Killer Croquettes | padkos | 16
s: Makes 20-25 pieces
t: Prep 20 min (plus cooling) | Cook 15 min | Chill overnight
> My path crossed with Chef Matt Manning in 2013 when I went to a OneIngredient Dinner with Sue-Anne Allen & Ilse Nel, two Masterchef contestants who I would later work with again. Matt’s One Ingredient for that dinner was Apricot. He infused a five course menu, of which we had to cook our own starts and mains, with Apricot. Later one we became good friends and Matt filmed with me for Padkos - We made these killer Mac & Cheese croquettes… and its insane! Golden, crispy, cheesy bites — perfect as a snack, starter or party treat
# For the Mac ’n Cheese
- 500 g macaroni
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 cup cream
- 1 cup milk
- 3 eggs
- 250 g Parmesan cheese, grated
- Oil, for deep-frying
- Panko breadcrumbs (or regular breadcrumbs)
- Flour, for coating
- 1 extra egg, beaten (for crumbing)
. Preheat oven to 180°C. Boil the macaroni in salted water until al dente. Drain and set aside.
. In a bowl, whisk together the cream, milk, and 3 eggs. Season with salt and pepper.
. Combine the cooked pasta with the cream mixture and grated Parmesan. Stir to coat evenly. Pour the pasta mixture into a baking tray and spread evenly.
. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until completely firm — ideally overnight.
. Once firm, cut the set macaroni mixture into cubes. Dredge each cube first in flour, then dip in the beaten egg, and coat with breadcrumbs.
. Heat oil in a deep pot or fryer to 180°C. Fry the croquettes in batches until golden brown. Remove and drain on paper towels. Serve immediately.

@ The Spider-Man Vetkoek | padkos | 17
s: 10
t: 3 hours
> I had the pleasure to cook with the late and great Lentswe Benghu, as well as going to Langa in Cape Town to try Umqombothi and Skaapkop. I’ve had skaapkop on the farm before, but the experience was amazing. In the vain of the offal, we then preceded to make Magwinya (Vetkoek) with a Peri Peri Chicken liver. It was a magical day.
# For the Vetkoek
- 1 kg cake flour
- 10 ml instant yeast
- 2 tbsp sugar
- Generous pinch of salt
- 600 ml lukewarm water
- Oil for greasing and frying (deep or shallow)
. On a flat surface, mix the flour, yeast, sugar, and salt. Gradually add the lukewarm water to form a manageable dough.
. Knead the dough until smooth and elastic, about 8 minutes. Lightly grease the dough with oil, cover, and let it rise in a warm place until doubled in size (about 2 hours).
. Gently punch down the dough and roll it into a long roll, approximately 4 cm in diameter. Pinch off pieces about 4 cm in size and flatten them to 1 cm thick. Let them rise slightly on a greased surface.
. Heat oil in a large pan or pot for deep or shallow frying, as preferred. Fry the vetkoek until golden brown on both sides. Drain on paper towels.
# For Peri-Peri Chicken Liver Filling
- 250 g chicken livers, rinsed in water
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 10 red chilies, deseeded and finely chopped
- 4 red peppers, finely chopped
- 100 ml red wine vinegar
- 30 g smoked paprika
. Sauté the onion, chilies, and red peppers over medium heat for 10-15 minutes until softened. Deglaze the pan with red wine vinegar.
. Add the smoked paprika and let the mixture simmer for 20 minutes. Blend in a food processor until smooth.
. Mix the peri-peri sauce with the raw chicken livers and let it marinate for a few minutes.
. Heat a pan with olive oil and fry the chicken livers. Season with salt and pepper.
. Slice open the vetkoek and fill with the peri-peri chicken livers. Serve warm and enjoy!

@ West Coast Mussels | padkos | 18
s: 6
t: 40 minutes
> Kobus van der Merwe from Wolfgat in Paternoster was one of my favourite guests in the Food truck. Kobus is generous of spirit and kind at heart. We foraged for mussels and seaweed and made West coast mussels in a beurre blanc sauce, as well as ‘hoenderboudjie’ - Kobus’ very clever bread sticks to mop up all the deliciousness. Enjoy this elegant West Coast dish — fresh, briny, and beautifully balanced.
# For the Mussels
- 1 kg fresh West Coast mussels, in shell
- 1 glass (±150 ml) Sauvignon Blanc
- A generous glug of olive oil
- A generous glug of cream
- 100 g Cape gooseberries (appelliefies)
- 100 g sea coral (or julienned celery as substitute)
- 100 g sea lettuce (or pea shoots as substitute)
- 100 g pickled beach greens (or cos lettuce leaves as substitute)
# For the Hoenderboudjie breadsticks
- Ready-made bread dough
- Sea salt
- Dried herbs (e.g., oregano, thyme)
. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Roll the dough into a 10 cm log, then thin out the ends to resemble a chicken drumstick (“hoenderbeentjie”). Sprinkle with sea salt and dried herbs. Bake for 12 minutes, or until golden brown and cooked through. Set aside.
. In a large pot, heat a good glug of olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the mussels, cover with a lid and steam until they begin to open and go quiet.
. Lift the lid and pour in the glass of Sauvignon Blanc. Cover again and steam for 2 minutes, just enough to cook off the alcohol. Strain the cooking liquid through a fine sieve and set aside.
. Allow the mussels to cool slightly, then remove from the shells. Return the mussels to the cooking liquid and warm through.
. Remove the mussels again, add a generous splash of cream to the liquid, stir, and heat gently. Set aside the creamy sauce.
. On each plate, arrange 5–6 mussels per portion. Add a few Cape gooseberries, sea coral (or celery), sea lettuce (or pea shoots), and pickled greens (or cos leaves).
. Spoon over a little of the creamy wine sauce. Serve immediately with warm hoenderbeentjiebroodjies on the side.

@ Prawn, Avo & Mango Salad | padkos | 19
s: 4
t: 20 min
> This summer dish featured in the second season of Padkos, and is a light, tropical salad packed with sweet mango, creamy avocado, and a zesty citrus dressing.
- 400 g cooked tiger prawns
- 2 mangos, peeled and cut into 2 cm cubes
- 2 avocados, cut into 2 cm cubes
- 2 limes, plus extra lime wedges for serving
- ⅓ cup coriander leaves, finely chopped
- 1 long red chilli, seeds removed and finely chopped
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp avocado oil or extra virgin olive oil
- 1 butter lettuce, leaves separated
- Micro herbs, to serve (optional)
. Peel and devein the prawns. Roughly chop half of them and leave the rest whole for garnish. In a large bowl, combine the chopped prawns, mango cubes, and avocado.
. Grate the zest of half a lime and juice both limes. In a small bowl, whisk together the lime zest and juice, coriander, chilli, honey, and oil. Season well with salt and pepper.
. Pour the dressing over the prawn mixture and toss gently to combine.
. Divide the butter lettuce leaves among serving plates and spoon the salad into each. Top with the remaining whole prawns, lime wedges, and micro herbs (if using). Serve immediately for a refreshing starter or light main.
! This salad pairs beautifully with a chilled glass of Sauvignon Blanc or a crisp rosé.
